This brief covers AI news from 2026-08-23 UTC.

Today's brief covers model launches, a key hire, an acquisition, and funding news.

## Grok 4.6 Now on Google Cloud Vertex AI

**What happened:** xAI's Grok 4.6 is now available on Google Cloud's Vertex AI, with a 500, 000-token context window and four reasoning levels. It's priced for enterprise adoption and accessible via Vertex Model Garden.

**Why it matters:** Developers can now use Grok 4.6 for long-running agents and coding tasks directly within Google Cloud, expanding deployment options.

[Source: bitcoinethereumnews.com](<https://bitcoinethereumnews.com/tech/xais-grok-4-6-launches-on-google-cloud-vertex-ai/>)

## Anthropic Hires TPU Creator Amir Salek

**What happened:** Anthropic confirmed on August 21 that Amir Salek, founder and head of Google's TPU program from 2013 to 2022, is joining its compute team. Anthropic is Google's largest external TPU tenant, with access to up to one million TPUs.

**Why it matters:** Hiring the architect behind the TPU signals Anthropic's push to shape its own silicon strategy, potentially reducing reliance on rented chips.

[Source: ainvest.com](<https://www.ainvest.com/news/anthropic-hires-man-built-tpu-million-chip-renter-draws-silicon-2608/>)

## OpenAI Acquires InstantDB Team

**What happened:** OpenAI acquired the team behind InstantDB, a real-time database platform serving over 17, 000 users and 400, 000 apps. Instant Cloud is being phased out, with support until August 2027.

**Why it matters:** OpenAI gains backend infrastructure expertise to support persistent state management for AI agents, a key requirement for agentic workflows.

[Source: cryptobriefing.com](<https://cryptobriefing.com/openai-acquires-instantdb-team/>)

## Anthropic Launches $1.5B Ode AI Consultancy

**What happened:** Anthropic, with Blackstone, Hellman &amp; Friedman, and Goldman Sachs, launched Ode, a $1.5 billion AI services operation. It embeds Claude engineers in midsized private-equity portfolio companies, with first customers in Q4 2026.

**Why it matters:** This creates a new revenue stream for Anthropic beyond API consumption and offers a turnkey way for enterprises to integrate Claude.

[Source: SaasRise](<https://www.saasrise.com/news/anthropic-launches-15b-ode-ai-consultancy-for-privateequity-portfolios-ab05c9c7-4add-4fee-b3c9-ff7efa789130>)

## Starcloud Raises $250M at $2.3B Valuation

**What happened:** Starcloud, an orbital data center startup, raised $250 million at a $2.3 billion valuation. Manhattan West led, with Nvidia and Cisco Investments joining. It's working with Nvidia on the Space-1 Vera Rubin Module.

**Why it matters:** Orbital data centers could provide AI compute capacity beyond Earth, a novel approach to scaling infrastructure.

[Source: Metapress](<https://metapress.net/business/2026/08/22/orbital-data-center-startup-starcloud-valued-at-2-3-billion-in-latest-funding/>)

## Blacksmith Raises $45M for AI CI Infrastructure

**What happened:** Blacksmith, a San Francisco startup building CI infrastructure for GitHub Actions, closed a $45 million Series B led by Peak XV Partners at a $550 million valuation. It also launched Codesmith, a cloud coding agent that auto-fixes CI failures.

**Why it matters:** As AI-generated code surges, faster CI and automated fixes address the new bottleneck in software delivery.

[Source: byteiota](<https://byteiota.com/blacksmith-ai-code-validation/>)

## Colorado AI Rules Face FTC Preemption Claim

**What happened:** Colorado is writing rules for the Automated Decision-Making Technology Act, with comments open until October 26. The FTC published a policy statement on July 1 asserting federal law may preempt state AI output mandates.

**Why it matters:** Developers building AI systems for housing, employment, and insurance face uncertain compliance requirements as state and federal rules clash.

[Source: Forkast](<https://forkast.news/colorado-is-writing-ai-transparency-rules-the-ftc-says-federal-law-may-override-them/>)
