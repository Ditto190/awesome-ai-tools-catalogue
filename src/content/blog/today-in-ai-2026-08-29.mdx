This brief covers AI news from 2026-08-29 UTC.

Open-weight models, big money for AI infrastructure, and a landmark FTC ruling shape today's news.

## Tencent open-sources Hy4 preview with 770B parameters

**What happened:** Tencent released and open-sourced Hy4 preview, a 770B-parameter MoE model with 49B active parameters and a 1M-token context window. It's available via WorkBuddy, CodeBuddy, Yuanbao, and API through Tencent Cloud TokenHub and OpenRouter.

**Why it matters:** Developers get a top-tier open model for coding and productivity, with free access for two weeks on WorkBuddy and CodeBuddy.

[Source: Tencent](<https://www.tencent.com/tencent-releases-and-open-sources-tencent-hy4-preview/>)

## Z.ai releases GLM-5.3 weights for coding and security

**What happened:** Z.ai released GLM-5.3 weights, a 756GB model with 256 routed experts and 1M-token context, under a custom license. It scored 28.3 on Terminal-Bench 3.0, up from 4.6 for GLM-5.2.

**Why it matters:** Developers can self-host a model built for long-running coding and vulnerability hunting, with support for Transformers, vLLM, and SGLang.

[Source: RuntimeWire](<https://runtimewire.com/article/zai-releases-glm-5-3-open-weights-coding-cyber-defense>)

## a16z raises $1.1B Machine Age fund for AI hardware

**What happened:** Andreessen Horowitz launched a $1.1B fund focused on AI infrastructure: chips, memory, data centers, and robots. The firm says it's time to 'accelerate the physical buildout of AI.'

**Why it matters:** More capital for hardware startups could ease supply constraints that bottleneck AI development.

[Source: TechCrunch](<https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/28/a16z-creates-a-1-1b-machine-age-fund-to-accelerate-the-physical-buildout-of-ai/>)

## Lambda raises $1B in debt to buy Nvidia chips

**What happened:** Lambda secured $1B in private short-dated debt, arranged by JP Morgan, to buy Nvidia AI chips that it will lease to Microsoft. This follows a $926M loan for GB300 GPUs.

**Why it matters:** Neoclouds are scaling GPU supply fast, giving developers more rental options for high-end chips.

[Source: TechCrunch](<https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/28/neocloud-lambda-secures-1b-in-debt-to-buy-more-chips/>)

## FTC narrows AI tool provider liability in Rytr vacatur

**What happened:** The FTC set aside its 2024 consent order against Rytr, establishing a three-part test for the means-and-instrumentalities theory. Providers are liable only if they supply deceptive materials, offer inherently deceptive products, or know of misuse.

**Why it matters:** Developers of dual-use AI tools face less legal exposure for how users deploy their products.

[Source: Forkast](<https://forkast.news/ftc-narrows-ai-tool-provider-liability-in-landmark-rytr-vacatur/>)

## AMD ROCm 10 delivers 3.3x inference uplift

**What happened:** AMD released ROCm 10 with ROCm.AI, a new developer experience. Systems with ROCm.AI show an average 3.3x increase in inference performance and 2.4x in training versus ROCm 7.

**Why it matters:** AMD is closing the software gap, making its GPUs a more viable option for running AI workloads.

[Source: Wccftech](<https://wccftech.com/amd-rocm-10-big-ai-updates-performance-gains/>)

## Huawei Cloud CodeArts Agent goes GA in Asia Pacific

**What happened:** Huawei Cloud CodeArts Agent is now commercially available in Asia Pacific, with Basic and Professional editions. It includes Agent Team with 16 specialized agents and 30+ Huawei engineering skills.

**Why it matters:** Enterprise developers in the region get an agentic coding tool that handles complex, long-duration tasks.

[Source: PR Newswire](<https://www.prnewswire.com/apac/news-releases/huawei-cloud-codearts-agent-now-available-across-asia-pacific-bringing-agentic-ai-to-software-development-302862642.html>)
