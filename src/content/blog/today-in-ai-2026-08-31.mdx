This brief covers AI news from 2026-08-31 UTC.

Today's brief covers major acquisitions, funding rounds, and new model releases.

## Stripe acquires OpenRouter for $7B, 5x May valuation

**What happened:** Stripe has acquired OpenRouter for $7 billion, five times its valuation from May, according to a report from NBTC News.

**Why it matters:** OpenRouter is a key gateway for developers to access multiple AI models; Stripe's ownership could change pricing and access for AI builders.

[Source: NBTC News](<https://news.nbtc.finance/stripe-acquires-openrouter-for-7b-five-times-its-may-valuation/>)

## Nvidia in talks to buy Hugging Face for $12.9B

**What happened:** The Information reported Nvidia agreed to acquire Hugging Face for $12.9 billion, but neither company has confirmed; Business Insider says talks haven't produced a signed deal.

**Why it matters:** Hugging Face is the largest open model hub; Nvidia ownership could reshape model distribution and inference routing for developers.

[Source: AI2Work](<https://ai2.work/blog/nvidia-s-12-9b-hugging-face-deal-puts-open-ai-under-chip-control>)

## SoftBank offers OpenAI $5.5B in warrants for data center commitment

**What happened:** SB Energy, a SoftBank subsidiary, has offered OpenAI stock warrants valued at roughly $5.5 billion to secure its long-term commitment as a tenant in its data center campuses.

**Why it matters:** The deal ties OpenAI's compute infrastructure to SoftBank's buildout, potentially affecting OpenAI's costs and capacity for developers.

[Source: Crypto Briefing](<https://cryptobriefing.com/softbank-openai-data-center-warrants/>)

## Tencent's Hy4 preview: 770B params, Apache 2.0, $0.83/M input

**What happened:** Tencent released Hy4 preview, a 770B-parameter MoE model with 49B active, 1M-token context, under Apache 2.0, priced at $0.83 per million input tokens on OpenRouter.

**Why it matters:** Open-weight, low-cost coding model with permissive license gives developers a viable alternative to proprietary APIs for agentic coding.

[Source: byteiota](<https://byteiota.com/tencent-hy4-open-weights-model-benchmarks-and-access/>)

## AWS open-sources Kiro Crew for async coding agents

**What happened:** Amazon announced Kiro Crew, an open-source system for running multiple Kiro coding agents asynchronously, with shared memory, scheduled jobs, and MCP integration.

**Why it matters:** Developers can now run coding agents without babysitting, enabling parallel tasks and unattended workflows.

[Source: InfoQ](<https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/08/kiro-crew-coding-agents/>)

## Huawei Cloud launches CodeArts Agent in Singapore

**What happened:** Huawei Cloud commercially launched CodeArts Agent in Singapore, an AI coding tool with 16 specialized agents covering the full software development lifecycle.

**Why it matters:** Expands AI coding tool availability in Asia, offering an alternative for enterprises in the region.

[Source: IT Brief Asia](<https://itbrief.asia/story/huawei-cloud-launches-the-codearts-agent-in-singapore>)

## Perplexity seeks $30B valuation with Nvidia investment

**What happened:** Perplexity AI is reportedly in talks to raise a new round at a $30 billion valuation, with Nvidia negotiating to participate.

**Why it matters:** Nvidia's potential investment could secure compute for Perplexity's inference-heavy search, affecting AI search competition.

[Source: i10x.ai](<https://i10x.ai/news/perplexity-ai-30-billion-valuation-nvidia>)
